# Project Assignment 2: Bad Poets Society

## Assignment Overview

In this assignment you are required to build a **poem generator**. You may use any of the approaches covered in the lectures that you like.

## Assignment Subtasks

The assignment can be broken down into the following subtasks:

### 1. Gather an Inspiring Set for Poems

To inform the development of your poem generator, start by gathering an **inspiring set of poems**. Remember from Ritchie's definition of an inspiring set that it does not require that it be recorded in a database. Your system does **not** have to read a file containing your inspiring set, but the inspiring set should provide examples of the types of poems that you would like your system to produce, even if they are not used directly.

You should **document your inspiring set in your logbook**.

To get started, you may wish to explore the following resources:

- [Poetry.com](https://www.poetry.com/)
- [Poetry Foundation](https://www.poetryfoundation.org/)
- [PoemHunter.com](https://www.poemhunter.com/)



> **Source clarification:** The screenshot lists *Poetry.net*, but the supplied URL is *Poetry.com*. These are not assumed to be the same website.

### 2. Implement a Poem Generator

Implement a poem generator using an approach of your choosing, e.g., **templates, grammars, Markov chains**, etc. The techniques covered in the tutorials so far should provide you with good starting points for implementing such a system.

You are **not limited** to the techniques covered in class; e.g., you may use pretrained models, but you are expected to think critically about your choice and how this impacts your inspiring set.

You may implement your system in either **JavaScript or Python**. You may use external libraries to implement your poem generator, e.g., **RiTa, Tracery, spaCy, TensorFlow**, etc.

Think carefully about the **stopping criteria** for your poem generator, i.e., when should your system stop producing a poem, and what do you want your system to output at the end of a run? For example:

- Should your system generate a poem of a fixed length?
- Should it generate a poem of a particular style?

You should **document your initial idea** for your poetry generator in your **logbook** and update this as you implement your system based on challenges faced or new goals you wish to achieve.

### 3. Use a Generative AI to Present Your Poems

Decide how you want to present **at least three poems generated by your system**, e.g.:

- Visually, using text and images;
- Orally, using spoken word;
- Traditionally, in the form of an anthology.

Use a **Generative AI** to help you present your poems. Examples include:

- A **text-to-image generator**, such as Stable Diffusion, DALL-E, or Midjourney, to produce imagery for your poems or verses;
- A **text-to-speech generator**, such as Speechify or ElevenLabs, to produce a spoken version of your poem.

You are **not limited** to these options. You may use the generated material as-is or as the basis for your presentation and augment it, e.g., add the text of your poetry to an image.

Think about the **presentation of your poems as part of your creative system**, as a way of increasing the perceived value of a poem.

## Deliverables

### Deliverable 1: PDF Report

Submit a **PDF document containing a report (maximum 4 pages)** using the **given template**.

The report should:

- Explain your process and reflections on developing your creative system.
- Give a brief, step-by-step account of how you tackled the tasks above.
- Discuss your thoughts on the process, such as how you decided on your inspiring set.
- **Conclude with a reflection on how to evaluate the creativity of your system**, or, if you attempted an evaluation, reflect on how it went.
- Include a reflection on the process of **using Generative AI** for the task it was used for.

### Deliverable 2: Source Code ZIP

Submit a **ZIP archive of your source code and data files** for your creative system.

The ZIP must include:

- Source code;
- Relevant data files;
- A **README** describing the system requirements for compiling and running your system.

Additional notes from the assignment:

- If you develop your system in the **P5.js online editor**, download and submit a copy of the source files.
- If you develop your system in **Google Colab**, include in the archive the data files on which it depends.

### Deliverable 3: Poetry Presentation ZIP

Submit a **ZIP archive containing the presentation of at least three poems**:

- Each poem must be **generated by your poem generator**.
- Each poem must be **presented using Generative AI**.
- The archive must also contain the **complete texts of each presented poem**.
- The report must clearly explain **how Generative AI was used** to produce the presentation of the poems.
